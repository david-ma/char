Introduction

My name is Char (they/he), and this is Space For Your Mind, your new favourite trans-owned and operated inclusive cleaning and organising service.

I first entered the cleaning and organising industry after my mum died in 2022, and I was left as the executor of her estate. As I was sorting through generations of sentimental memories, I started to closely consider the human relationship to collecting, consumption, and waste. Why do we hold on to things? What compels us to buy things we don’t need?

Add this to my lifelong interest in hoarding disorder, and you have the foundation of my business. I am here to help people confront their relationship with their belongings, reshape their living spaces, and help to make sense of their habits in the context of mental health. I also LOVE a cleaning transformation and one of my favourite things to do is restore a challenging space!

I am specifically here to help the queer and neurodivergent community - I have noticed there is a big gap when it comes to organising and cleaning services for us, and I want to fill it! I am a trans and autistic individual myself and my solutions come from lived experience. My services are accessible for everybody, regardless of gender, background, or age. This is a safe space and you can be happy in the knowledge that you will be listened to and respected at every point in the process.

I’m really looking forward to meeting and helping you!

Decluttering and Organising

My decluttering and organising service is for you if you are having trouble creating and maintaining organisation in any part of your living space. I can help you with one area (like a pantry or wardrobe), or we can dig right in and do the whole room - or the whole home! I start by chatting with you about your goals for the space, and any accessibility needs or special requests you may have. Then we can either work together decluttering, where I help you choose items to donate or discard, or you can sit back and let me organise your space without letting anything go.

Decluttering and organising can be done over several sessions on an ongoing basis, or in one session – this all depends upon the size of the space and complexity of the task, and we will discuss a suitable timeframe in our initial consultation. I can also add on a deep clean of the home when the organising is finished, for that finishing touch! I am able to facilitate rubbish removal as well as small donations to op-shops (based on suitability of items and at the op-shop’s discretion).

Deep Cleaning

Has your home or living space really got away from you? I understand, and I am here to help. If you are worried that your home has got to the point where it is no longer sanitary, or if you are simply overwhelmed by the task ahead, I can provide a deep, industrial clean for you. This can be for one room that you just can’t tackle alone, such as the kitchen, or it can be for the whole house. I provide a discreet, kind, and compassionate service, with my only goal being to make you feel safe and comfortable in your own space again.

Hoarding and Extreme Clutter

Hoarding disorder is a complicated, isolating condition, but it is more common than you might think. If yourself or someone you know is struggling to let items go, while bringing more and more items into their already cluttered living space, seeking external help can be highly beneficial. I have extensive experience working with people who struggle with hoarding, and I am not here to simply come into your home and throw everything away. I am here to chat openly and honestly with you about your items and collections, what they mean to you, and what we might do with those items which are no longer serving a purpose to you. I aim to provide a holistic, ongoing approach to my hoarding clients and guide them through the difficult, yet necessary, process of letting items go. I also provide ongoing sessions and follow ups, to ensure the space is being maintained and to help with any extra cleaning and re-organising that may be necessary in the future.

Deceased Estate

Being left as an executor after someone passes away is one of the most stressful things one can go through - having been in this position myself, I know first hand. Sorting a loved one’s home after they have passed is emotional and overwhelming, but I can provide the helping hand that you need. I can help you sort through belongings, pack them up, and help you with the logistics of transportation and storage. I can also provide a full home clean when we have sorted all the remaining belongings, to get the house spick and span and ready for the market, or for the next occupant. Consider me a “one stop shop” for your deceased estate needs, and relieve the pressure of your work as an executor.

Online Help

Do you live outside of my service area, but would really like some help with your home? Even if I can’t be there personally, I can still help you get started. Through video calls, I will provide an initial consultation to learn about your situation and understand where you are struggling – that might be with too much clutter, keeping on top of regular cleaning, or bringing too many items into your home. From there, I will write you a unique plan with goals, strategies, and checklists to help you take control of your space. With regular check ins to keep you accountable, help might not be as far away as you think.

Downsizing and Relocation

Have you got an elderly relative moving into aged care? Or perhaps your children have moved out and you are looking to move into a smaller, more manageable home? Whatever your circumstances, I can help you with the difficult task of fitting a lifetime’s worth of memories into a much smaller space. Working together with you, we can sort through items, deciding what to keep, donate, sell, or discard, in a way that honours their significance and value.

I can also package in a full home clean to have the original home spotless to hit the market or to be ready for the next tenant.
